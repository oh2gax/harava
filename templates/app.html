Layer
The filter applies to the Map, the Day counts, the Watch list and Devices. Group same device: the changing addresses of one device (the same Bluetooth Mesh number, the same Google advertisement or day code of an Android device, or the same custom service and name) as one radio. Hide my devices: the radios of My devices (＋ My device on a radio's page, or on the full site). Travelled with me: heard at two of your stops at least {{ travel_min_m }} m apart (at N stops), or for minutes while you moved (same road). Remembered on the phone.
Map options
One pin per radio, where your phone heard it loudest: filled Bluetooth, rings Wi-Fi, bigger louder, the colour of its layer; glasses, trackers and other watch-list radios as icons, a separated tracker pulsing red. Tap a pin, then the card, for its details. ⌂ fits the day. Spread stacked pins: radios heard at the same spot are spread into a small flower so each can be tapped.
Day options
Timeline shows
The bars: hears per 10 minutes (Wi-Fi blue, Bluetooth green); the line: radios heard for the first time. With one layer chosen, only its radios count — for example Glasses & VR.
Devices options
Sort by
Search matches the name, address, maker, signature, layer, Identified as and company. Tags: N addr. one device with many addresses, at N stops with you at that many stops, same road alongside you for minutes, separated tracker, sit only, ★ my device. Tap a radio for its details.
Phone check options
Signal of a phone
At the start of a lesson the phones go into a box next to the teacher's phone, which runs Fieldwatch. Import its log, choose the day, set the window start to a moment when the phones are in the box and 2–5 minutes, then Auto-calibrate. Phones of My devices never count. A metal box blocks the signal; use cardboard or plastic.
Identification options
Harava's own identification database, on top of Fieldwatch's signatures: the Bluetooth SIG numbers and Harava's rules. Still unknown lists what the unidentified radios advertise{% if user.role == 'admin' %}; the Workbench sorts them and turns them into rules{% endif %}.
Imports options
Upload needs the Analyze permission. Each file tells what it brought: new; extends — a newer export of the rolling log (Fieldwatch exports everything still on the phone, so most rows were already stored); overlaps; duplicate — nothing new; sit update — a newer export of the same sit. Your imports are yours: share one with all users if others should see it (on the full site also with chosen users); admins see everyone's data. A log and a Sit of the same time work together; a KML of the same phone and time is switched off. Switch a file off to leave it out without deleting it; Re-read reads the kept original again.

About
What
{{ app_name }} — Finnish for rake: it rakes through what your phone heard. It reads the logs and Sit exports of the Fieldwatch Android app and shows every Wi-Fi access point and Bluetooth radio your phone heard, on a map and in lists, and names many that Fieldwatch leaves unidentified.
Author
Otso Laakso / OH2GAX
Data
Fieldwatch only listens: it never connects to anything. A pin is where your phone was when it heard the radio, not where the radio is.
